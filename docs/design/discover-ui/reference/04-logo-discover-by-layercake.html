How we got here

v1, hand-drawn. Loose c-o-v spacing, winking s and e, rounded "by layercake".
Alternative: Nunito Black. Heavier and tighter; louder at small sizes.
Alternative: Quicksand Bold. Lighter and more elegant; less playful.
How it's built"discover" is Fredoka SemiBold with the i's dot replaced by the spark; "by Layercake" is Poppins Medium. Both are outlined to SVG shapes (both fonts are open licence), so the files need no fonts and stay crisp at any size.
The spark is the only colourIt's the same spark as the AI glyph and Pip's headpiece, so the product, the assistant and the mascot share one symbol. Keep clear space of one spark-height on every side.
Don'tRecolour the letters orange, move or enlarge the spark, retype it in a font, outline it, or put the full lockup below 120px wide.
