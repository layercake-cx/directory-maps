Geometry

Block ratio 1.2 : 1 (wide), seen from front-left with a 14u right side. Spark: centred over the top, 6u right of centre, gap above head ≈ ⅓ face height. Eyes on the face layer's upper half; mouth sits just above the water line. Arms leave from the water layer; legs from the ⅓ points of the base. Orange trainers with cream toe caps and soles. Limbs are always pip-ink.
Never changes: layer order and relative thickness, cream map top, orange spark, face placement, trainers, dark limbs, proportions.
Can change: eyes, brows, mouth, arm gestures, stance, props (magnifier, tablet, map, briefcase, bubble, chart). Props are separate layers.

Still needed for sign-off: true side and back views, and a walk cycle turnaround.
